Light Of Books - Delete Account

This page explains how users of the Light Of Books app can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Light Of Books account, please contact us using the support email address below.

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your account.
  2. Use the subject: Delete My Light Of Books Account.
  3. Include your registered email address or phone number.
  4. We will verify the request and process the account deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is approved, we will delete the account information associated with your account, including your authentication account and user profile information stored by the app, where applicable.

  • Account information
  • Email address, where applicable
  • Phone number, where applicable
  • User profile information
  • Other account-associated data stored by the app

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate purposes. If any information must be retained, it will only be kept for the period required for that purpose.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are normally processed after verification of the request.
